- Include a chapter title, module/week context, an anchored contents sidebar, and a visible link back to the [course contents](index.html). Adapt navigation for smaller screens and include print styles.
- Embed rendered equations and static SVG diagrams. Keep long equations and tables within their own scroll regions; avoid page-wide horizontal overflow. Use legible diagram layouts at desktop and phone widths.
- Keep [index.html](index.html) aligned with the approved curriculum. Link only to authored chapters, mark unavailable chapters clearly, and update chapter availability when a lesson is added. Availability is not learner mastery.
- Preserve the existing curriculum, instructional depth and source discipline. This format preference does not authorise simulations or quizzes; those remain opt-in under AGENTS.md.
- Apply the tutor voice in AGENTS.md §4A: connect the reasoning in prose, define terms and symbols before use, explain why important calculation steps follow, and interpret their results. Keep authoring and verification notes in PROGRESS.md rather than interrupting the lesson.

The curriculum remains the authority for starting level, subject scope and sequence. No additional learner background or mastery is inferred from these presentation preferences.
