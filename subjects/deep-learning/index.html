Deep learning / Course contents

Architectural intuition and engineering judgment

Intermediate machine learning to deep learning · 12-week core course

Understanding the choices behind a network

Learn how neural networks work, why their structures suit particular problems, and how to judge their fit to an unfamiliar application.

The course moves from learned features and gradients to convolution, memory, attention and architectural composition. The emphasis is on mechanisms, assumptions, alternatives and failure cases.
